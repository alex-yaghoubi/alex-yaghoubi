### How I approach product & engineering

- **Start with failure modes.** Define recovery paths, permissions, and operational constraints before celebrating the happy path.
- **Make asset movement explicit.** Transaction states, approvals, and reconciliation should be understandable and auditable.
- **Connect strategy to execution.** Translate ambiguous opportunities into testable decisions, milestones, and measurable outcomes.
- **Design for the operators.** Reliability, observability, and incident response are part of the product experience.
